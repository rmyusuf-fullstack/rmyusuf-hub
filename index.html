APPROACH

I DON'T EDIT
EVERY VIDEO THE SAME WAY.

Every video has something it needs to communicate. I start there — understanding the message, the audience, and the feeling the video needs to leave behind.

01

UNDERSTAND

I identify what the video needs to say, who it is speaking to, and what should remain after the viewer finishes watching.

02

SHAPE

I shape pacing, structure, visuals, sound, and movement around that purpose rather than forcing the same editing style onto every project.

03

REFINE

Once the story works, I refine the details — from transitions and motion to color, effects, and sound — so everything supports the final result.
